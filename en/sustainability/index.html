Commitment to Sustainability

Haruharu Television Group is committed to achieving sustainability through the following initiatives:

Environmental Protection Efforts

  • Waste Reduction
    • Reducing waste during activities, including those by subsidiaries
    • Conducting trash collection activities by subsidiaries

Encouraging Environmental Conservation

Encouraging members to engage in environmental conservation

SDGs Initiatives

Goals

  • 3. Good Health and Well-being for All
  • 4. Quality Education for All
  • 11. Sustainable Cities and Communities
  • 16. Peace, Justice, and Strong Institutions

Specific Activities

  • Health and Well-being (3)
    • Disseminating information through collaboration with local governments
  • Sustainable Cities and Communities (11)
    • Protecting lives through local government information and disaster prevention content
    • Providing disaster prevention information centered around the “Haruharu TV Disaster Reduction Project”
    • Sharing experiences from the Kumamoto Earthquake on a dedicated website, accumulating and presenting field survey results
  • Peace, Justice, and Strong Institutions (16)
    • Creating and disseminating responsible content
    • Planning participation in SDGs media activities
  • Quality Education (4)
    • Providing quizzes on general knowledge and disaster prevention on our website
    • Planning to offer a platform for easy learning of general knowledge through Haruharu Academy*1
      *1 Not an educational institution defined by the School Education Act, nor a corporation defined by the Companies Act.
